Xi Wu once gazed across the long night,  
not knowing the far shore was this dawn.  
A needle of spring water sheathes the frost blade,  
half a year of dusk exchanged for an old robe.

Tonight I look back and pity my former self,  
tomorrow, landing, I will remember that night.  
If you ask when the jade is finally polished—  
before the mist clears, it is the broken bridge.
